What these measurements can tell you

Battery percentages are rounded. A discharge estimate needs at least 10 uninterrupted minutes and a drop of 2 percentage points. Fast drain uses 15-minute windows at your selected threshold.

Charging, missing observations, sleep or clock changes, battery increases, and collector restarts break the discharge timeline. The plot leaves these breaks visible.

CPU time is evidence of work, not a measurement of per-app energy. Display brightness, GPU work, networking, and temperature can also affect battery use. A foreground app is a sampled observation, not a continuous activity log.

The comparison window is the preceding period of equal length. It may contain a different workload. Reports are deterministic and stay on this Mac.
